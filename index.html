03 · Feature2026

Paper trading with guardrails

Turns an agent's BUY, SELL, or HOLD into a paper order, but only after it passes hard risk checks.

PythonAlpaca APIRisk management

Problem

An LLM's decision is a paragraph of text. Acting on it blindly could mean trading on a stale or misread call.

What I built

  • Paper-only Alpaca client for now; the goal is to make it work with live trading next
  • Limits per stock, total exposure, daily loss, and orders per day
  • Strict reading of saved decisions, refusing anything unclear

Result

Every order shows each check it passed or failed, and the user still places it with a click. Nothing trades on its own.

05 · AnalysisIn progress

Decision scorecard

Grades every saved BUY and SELL call against what the price actually did next.

PythonpandasBacktesting

Problem

It's easy to remember the good calls. Without a record, there's no honest way to know if the agents are any good.

What I built

  • Compares each call with simply holding SPY
  • Checks 5, 20, and 60 trading days later
  • Lists HOLD and unclear calls without grading them

Result

Hit rates are only reported after 30 graded calls, since fewer than that is mostly luck.
